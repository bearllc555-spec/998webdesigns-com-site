## The design service — $5,998 flat

### What it is

A handcrafted custom site (not a template) designed around the customer's business. Standard package:

- **Up to 6 pages**
- **~500 MB storage**
- **Unmetered business traffic**
- **Domain included** (with 10-year hosting; .com / .net / .org)
- Mobile-optimized, click-to-call, click-to-map, contact form

### Delivery SLA

- **7 business days** from the moment the deposit clears.
- **14 business days** for full-stack builds (3 or more add-ons).
- Add-ons go live within **1–3 business days** of site delivery.

### What's NOT included (explicit boundaries)

- **Full copywriting from scratch.** Customers bring their copy and photos; design tunes structure and layout. If the customer has nothing, that's a separate creative engagement.
- **Unlimited revision rounds.** Defined review window after delivery. Beyond that, edits move to the per-edit pricing (see below).
- **Logos and brand identity systems.** Favicon and type choices are part of the build; a full brand book is not.
- **Hyper-local SEO, GBP management, blog writing, chatbots, email automation.** These are add-ons (see "Growth modules" below), priced separately, optional.
- **A guarantee of more revenue.** The site is somewhere credible to land; it does not fix a broken sales process or unanswered phones.

## Pricing

### Design fee — $5,998

Flat. Paid on a 50/40/10 schedule:

| Milestone | % | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Checkout (enter the queue) | 50% | $2,999.00 |
| After design approval / dev start | 40% | $2,399.20 |
| Launch and handover | 10% | $599.80 |

The design clock starts when the 50% deposit clears (bank transfers may take a few business days).

**Promo codes** are channel-specific and take a percentage off the design fee only — not hosting, not add-ons. Customers enter codes on the lead form.

### Hosting — pick one after the first 90 days

The 50% deposit includes the **first 90 days of hosting free** (worth $294). After day 90, the customer picks:

- **Month-to-month lease — $98/mo.** Cancel anytime before day 91 and no hosting charge applies. Upgrade to 10-year hosting at any later point for $2,996.
- **10-year hosting — $2,996 one-time.** Includes domain registration (.com, .net, .org). Begins when that payment clears.

**Hosting math gotcha (disclosed up front):** Past monthly payments don't apply toward the 10-year price. The customer is told this before they pick — not surprised by it later.

### Edits

- **Free for the first three months** (the dial-in window).
- **$10 each thereafter, $50 minimum top-up.** Submitted via form; $10 deducted per edit from the prepaid balance.

### Payment methods

- **Card** is default.
- **Bank transfer** is available and avoids the 3% card fee on the amount due that day.

## The four-step process

1. **Fill out the form** (5 steps, ~5 minutes). Tell us about the business — who it serves, what it does, what the site should do for it. Add-ons can be selected here so they're scoped into one timeline.
2. **Pay through Stripe.** Pick monthly or 10-year hosting, then pay the 50% design deposit. First 90 days of hosting are free.
3. **We build the site** in 7 business days (14 for full-stack). Add-on setup runs in parallel.
4. **Approve and go live.** Customer approves the draft, we launch same day. 10% milestone is due at handover. No surprise line items.

## What comes next (post-launch)

- **Launch & done.** Site is live, hosted, and the customer's. Edits are $10 each. Nothing else required.
- **Keep growing.** Selected add-ons go live within 1–3 business days of the site. New ones can be added any time — each compounds the last.

## The intake form (`/start`)

Five short steps, marketed as **"Detailed by design. The better the brief, the better the site."**

1. **About you** — name, company (optional), email, phone (optional), best way to reach (email / phone / text)
2. **Your business**
3. **The project**
4. **Add-ons**
5. **Logistics**

The form is positioned as both the qualifier (gating who can reasonably buy at $5,998) and the design brief (the input the designer works from). The depth is part of the value proposition — better brief, better site.
