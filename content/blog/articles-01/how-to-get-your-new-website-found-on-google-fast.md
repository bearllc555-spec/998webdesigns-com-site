# How to get a brand-new contractor website found on Google faster

*A new site is not broken just because it is invisible at first. Here is how to speed up indexing and start ranking sooner.*

You launched a new site and searched for it and found nothing. That is normal, not a defect. New sites take time to be discovered, indexed, and ranked - Google has to find them, understand them, and decide they are trustworthy. But there are concrete things that speed it up, and skipping them leaves your site sitting invisible longer than it needs to.

## First, understand the timeline

A brand-new site typically takes weeks to start appearing and longer to rank well. This is not your site failing - it is Google's normal process. Patience is part of the strategy. That said, you can accelerate the discovery and indexing steps significantly.

## What speeds it up

1. **Submit your site to Google Search Console.** Free. Verify your site, submit your sitemap, and request indexing for key pages. This tells Google your site exists instead of waiting for it to stumble across it.
2. **Connect your Google Business Profile to the site.** The profile is already trusted; linking it gives Google a path to your new pages.
3. **Get a few links pointing to the site.** Your social profiles, local directories, supplier or association listings. Links are how Google discovers and trusts new sites.
4. **Make sure the site is technically findable.** No accidental "noindex" settings, a working sitemap, clean internal linking so Google can crawl every page.
5. **Publish real, specific content.** Service pages and town pages with genuine substance get indexed and ranked faster than thin placeholder pages.

## What slows it down

- Leaving the site disconnected from Search Console, so Google takes longer to find it.
- A site accidentally set to block search engines (more common than you would think on new launches).
- Thin pages with little real content.
- No links from anywhere, so Google has no path in.

## Set expectations honestly

Even done perfectly, ranking for competitive local searches takes time. The free Google Business Profile often produces calls faster than the new site ranks, which is exactly why you do both - the profile carries you while the site climbs.

## How we handle it

When we build a site, it ships technically sound - crawlable, with a clean sitemap and proper structure - so it is ready to be found the day it launches rather than fighting hidden settings. Getting it into Search Console and linked from your profile is the fast first step after launch, and it is straightforward to do.
