# What to look for when hiring someone to build your contractor website

*Most web designers have never built for a trade business. Here is how to tell who understands local lead generation from who just makes things look nice.*

Hiring someone to build your site is hard because almost everyone says yes and almost no one specializes in what a plumber or roofer actually needs. A beautiful site that does not generate calls is a failure dressed up as a success. Here is how to tell the difference before you pay.

## The core question

Ask any candidate: "How will this site get me more calls?" A designer focused on aesthetics will talk about colors and layouts. One who understands trades will talk about local search, service pages, town targeting, mobile speed, and conversion. You want the second answer.

## What actually matters for a trade site

1. **They understand local SEO.** Service pages, town pages, Google Business Profile - if they have never heard these terms, they build brochures, not lead machines.
2. **They build mobile-first.** Most of your homeowners are on a phone. A designer who treats mobile as an afterthought is building for the wrong device.
3. **They care about speed.** Fast hosting, optimized images. A gorgeous slow site loses calls.
4. **They will give you clear ownership.** You own the domain and the site. If the deal traps you, that is a red flag.
5. **They have a clear price and timeline.** Vague pricing and "a few weeks" usually mean change orders and delays.

## The red flags

- A price that seems too cheap - you will pay later in fixes or lock-in.
- No portfolio of real local-business sites.
- Reluctance to let you own your domain.
- Endless monthly fees with no clear deliverable.
- All talk of design, none of leads.

## The questions to ask

- Who owns the site and domain when it is done?
- One-time fee or ongoing subscription?
- What does an edit cost after launch?
- How fast does it load on a phone?
- What is the exact delivery date?
- Can I see local trade sites you have built?
