# What is local SEO, and does my plumbing or roofing business need it?

*Local SEO is just the work of showing up when someone nearby searches for what you sell. Here is what it actually involves, in plain terms, with no agency jargon.*

"Local SEO" is one of those phrases agencies use to sound technical. Stripped of the jargon, it means one thing: showing up when someone near you searches for what you do. For a plumber or roofer, that is the whole game - your customers are within driving distance, and they are searching with intent.

## The three places you can show up

1. **The map pack.** The three businesses with map pins at the top of local results. This is where most "plumber near me" clicks go.
2. **The regular results.** The blue links below the map - where service pages and town pages rank.
3. **Your own Google Business Profile.** The panel that appears when someone searches your name directly.

Local SEO is the work of earning a spot in all three.

## What it actually involves

- **A complete, active Google Business Profile** - the single biggest lever, and free.
- **Consistent name, address, and phone** everywhere your business appears online.
- **A website with real service and town pages** so you can rank for specific searches.
- **A steady flow of recent reviews** with specifics.
- **Fast, mobile-friendly pages**, because that is how Google evaluates you and how homeowners search.
- **Mentions and listings** on directories homeowners and Google both trust.

None of it is mysterious. It is mostly doing the obvious things consistently, which is exactly why so few small contractors do it well.

## Does your business need it?

If your customers are local and they search before they hire - which describes nearly every plumber and roofer - then yes. The only contractors who can skip it are the ones already booked solid with referrals and uninterested in growing.

For everyone else, local SEO is not optional marketing. It is being findable at the moment a homeowner has a problem you solve.

## Where to start

Claim and complete your Google Business Profile first - free and fast. Get your name, address, and phone identical everywhere. Then build the site with the service and town pages that let you rank for specific searches. The profile wins the immediate call; the site wins the searches the profile cannot reach.
