# What does a good plumbing website actually look like?

*"Good" is not about flashy design. Here are the concrete traits of a plumbing site that gets found, builds trust, and rings the phone.*

When a plumber says they want a "good website," they usually picture something that looks impressive. But good, for a trade site, is not about flash - it is about a homeowner finding you, trusting you, and calling. A plain site that does those three things beats a beautiful one that does not. Here is what good actually looks like.

## It loads fast on a phone

The first trait, because it gates all the others. A homeowner with a leak is on their phone. If the site takes more than a couple of seconds, none of the rest matters - they are already gone.

## It answers "can you help me" in five seconds

The moment it loads, a good site makes three things obvious: what you do, where you work, and how to call. No hunting, no scrolling, no guessing.

## It has a page for each job you want

Not one "Services" page - separate pages for drain cleaning, water heaters, leak repair, repiping, emergency service. Each one can rank for the homeowner searching that exact problem.

## It proves you are legitimate

License number, years in business, insurance, real photos of real jobs, recent reviews. A homeowner about to let you into their home wants to feel safe first.

## It targets your towns

Pages that name the places you serve, so you rank for "[service] in [town]" - the searches that bring local, ready-to-hire homeowners.

## The phone number is everywhere and tappable

Above the fold, in the header, near every call to action, one-tap-to-dial on mobile. A good site never makes a ready homeowner hunt for how to reach you.

## It has one clear next step per page

Call, or fill a short form. Not five competing options. The site gently pushes every visitor toward the one action you want.

## What good does not require

- Animation and video that slow it down.
- A thirty-page sprawl nobody maintains.
- Cutting-edge design trends.
- A blog you will not keep up.

## The bottom line

Good is invisible when it works - the homeowner finds you, feels reassured, and calls, without ever thinking about the website itself. That is the bar we build to: fast, clear, credible, and structured to rank, not just to look nice in a screenshot.
