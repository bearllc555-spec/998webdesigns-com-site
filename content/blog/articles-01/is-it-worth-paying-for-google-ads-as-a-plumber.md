# Is it worth paying for Google Ads as a plumber or roofer?

*Ads can fill the calendar fast - and drain cash just as fast. Here is when paid search makes sense for a trade and when to build free traffic instead.*

Google Ads is tempting because it works immediately: pay, appear at the top, get calls today. For a contractor with an empty calendar, that is powerful. But ads are rented attention - the moment you stop paying, you vanish - and the clicks for trades can be expensive. Whether they are worth it depends entirely on your situation and your math.

## When ads make sense

1. **You need calls now.** New business, slow season, or filling a sudden gap. Ads deliver immediately while slower free channels build.
2. **Your job values are high.** A roofer whose average job is several thousand dollars can absorb a high cost-per-click and still profit. The math works when one job covers many clicks.
3. **You can track and answer.** Ads are wasted if the calls go unanswered or you cannot tell which ones came from the ad. You need a system.

## When ads are a money pit

- **Your site does not convert.** Paying for clicks that land on a slow or thin site is pouring money into a leaky bucket. Fix the site first.
- **You cannot answer the calls.** An ad-driven call that rings out is money set on fire.
- **You treat it as a substitute for free channels.** Ads on top of a strong free presence are leverage. Ads instead of one are a permanent tax.

## The expensive mistake most contractors make

Running ads before fixing the foundation. The ad brings a ready homeowner to a site that does not convert, or a phone that does not get answered, and the budget evaporates with nothing to show. Ads amplify whatever you already have - if your conversion is broken, ads just help you lose money faster.

## The right order

1. **Build the free foundation** - Google Business Profile, reviews, a site that converts. This is the asset you own and it does not vanish when you stop paying.
2. **Make sure calls get answered** - a visible number, and an AI receptionist for the ones you miss.
3. **Then layer ads on top** to accelerate, knowing the traffic they buy will actually convert.

## The bottom line

Ads are a fine accelerator and a terrible foundation. For most plumbers and roofers, the smart sequence is to build the free, owned channels first - so that when you do run ads, every dollar lands on a site and a phone system that turn clicks into booked jobs. We build the conversion foundation that makes paid traffic worth buying.
