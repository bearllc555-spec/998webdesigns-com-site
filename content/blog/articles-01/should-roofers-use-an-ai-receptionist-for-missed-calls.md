# Missed calls are costing roofers jobs. Is an AI receptionist the fix?

*A roofer on a roof cannot answer the phone. The caller dials the next roofer. Here is the real cost of missed calls and the options for catching them.*

Here is a number that should bother every roofing owner: a large share of inbound calls to home-service businesses go unanswered, and most of those callers do not leave a voicemail. They just dial the next roofer on the list. The lead you paid for, or earned, is gone in the time it takes to climb down a ladder.

The work that makes you money is the same work that makes you miss calls. That is the trap.

## The real cost of a missed call

A roof is a high-ticket job. If your average job is several thousand dollars and you miss even a couple of qualified calls a week, the annual cost is not a rounding error - it is a hire you could have afforded, or a truck you could have added. Missed calls are not an inconvenience. They are the leak in the bucket.

## The options, honestly

**1. Hire a receptionist.** Best human experience, highest cost. Hard to justify until your call volume is steady enough to keep one busy. Also off the clock at night and on weekends - when storm-damage calls spike.

**2. An answering service.** Cheaper than full-time, but the operators do not know roofing, so they take a message and you still call back later - by which point the homeowner has often moved on.

**3. An AI receptionist.** Answers every call instantly, day or night, in a natural voice. It can capture the name, address, and problem, answer common questions ("do you do insurance claims?"), book an estimate, and text you the details immediately. No call goes to a dead voicemail.

**4. Do nothing and call back fast.** Free, and viable if you are disciplined about returning every missed call within minutes. Most owners are not, through no fault of their own - they are on a roof.

## Where AI fits and where it does not

An AI receptionist is strong at the thing roofers lose most: the first response. It never sleeps, never lets a call ring out, and turns a 9pm storm call into a booked morning estimate instead of a missed opportunity.

It is not a replacement for you on the complex conversation - the insurance negotiation, the nervous homeowner who needs reassurance. It is the net that catches the call so you can have that conversation at all, instead of never knowing it happened.
