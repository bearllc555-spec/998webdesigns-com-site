# 7 roofing website mistakes that quietly cost you jobs

*Your roofing site might look fine and still lose homeowners before they call. Here are the seven mistakes we see most - and how to spot them on your own site.*

A roofing website can look perfectly professional and still leak leads. The problems are usually not ugly design - they are quiet friction points that send a ready-to-call homeowner to the next roofer. Here are the seven we see most.

## 1. The phone number is hard to find

A homeowner with storm damage is not going to hunt. If your number is not visible the second the page loads - and tap-to-call on mobile - you are losing the most urgent, highest-intent visitors.

## 2. No proof you are legitimate

Roofing attracts storm-chasers and fly-by-night crews, so homeowners are wary. No license number, no insurance mention, no real photos, no manufacturer certifications - and the careful homeowner moves on to someone who shows them.

## 3. Stock photos instead of real jobs

A generic photo of a roof from a stock library reads as empty. Real before-and-after shots of your crew's work do more to close a job than any amount of polished copy.

## 4. Slow loading on phones

Most roofing searches happen on a phone, often right after a storm on a weak signal. A site stuffed with huge unoptimized images takes too long, and the homeowner taps back.

## 5. No service-specific pages

"Roofing" is not a search. "Roof replacement," "storm damage repair," and "roof inspection" are. A single catch-all page cannot rank for the specific jobs homeowners actually search for.

## 6. No mention of insurance claims

A huge share of roofing work runs through insurance. If your site never mentions that you help with claims, you lose the homeowner who is overwhelmed by the process and looking for a roofer who handles it.

## 7. A dead-end contact path

A form that goes to an inbox nobody checks, or a number that rings out with no AI receptionist to catch it, means the lead you earned evaporates. The path has to actually connect.

## The pattern underneath all seven

Every one of these is intent leaking out at the last step. The homeowner found you, was interested, and hit friction. A roofing site built around the jobs you want, fast on mobile, heavy on proof, with the phone always one tap away, plugs the leaks - which is exactly how we build them.
