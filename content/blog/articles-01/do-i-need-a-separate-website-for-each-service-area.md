# Do I need a separate page for each town I serve?

*Town pages are powerful for local ranking - and easy to do wrong. Here is when they help, when they hurt, and how to build them right.*

If you serve ten towns, should you have ten pages? It is one of the most common local SEO questions for contractors, and the answer is "yes, if you do them right - and no, if you do them the lazy way that gets sites penalized." The difference matters a lot.

## Why town pages work

When a homeowner searches "plumber in [town]," Google looks for pages clearly about that service in that town. A dedicated, genuine page for each town you serve lets you rank for those specific searches - which are some of the highest-intent searches a local contractor can capture.

## The trap: doormat pages

The lazy version is to make ten near-identical pages, swapping only the town name, with no real content. Google calls these "doorway pages" and treats them as spam. They do not rank, and they can hurt your whole site. A page that says "We are the best plumber in [Town], call us for plumber services in [Town]" ten times over is the wrong way and actively risky.

## How to do town pages right

1. **Make each genuinely different.** Mention local landmarks, neighborhoods, the specific kinds of jobs common in that area, real local reviews or projects.
2. **Only build pages for towns you actually serve.** Do not invent pages for places you will not drive to - it dilutes your site and misleads homeowners.
3. **Add real substance.** What you do in that town, why local homeowners hire you, actual proof. Enough that the page stands on its own as useful.
4. **Link them sensibly.** Connect town pages to relevant service pages and vice versa, so the site holds together.

## How many is right

For most contractors, a handful of pages for your core, highest-value towns beats a sprawl of thin pages for every place within fifty miles. Quality and genuine relevance win. Start with the towns that matter most to your business and do those well.

## The honest tradeoff

Town pages are real work to do properly - which is exactly why done-right ones give you an edge over competitors who either skip them or spam them.
